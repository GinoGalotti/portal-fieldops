BALTIMORE POLICE DEPARTMENT · CENTRAL DISTRICT · HOMICIDE
CASE FILE 2005-1147
Inner Harbor Death Cluster — three subjects, no apparent cause
LEAD INVESTIGATORDet. Raymond Pulaski, badge 4488
DATE OPENED17 October 2005
STATUSActive. No suspect. No COD on any subject.
PATTERN NOTEThree deaths in five days, all within 400m of Inner Harbor waterfront. All three subjects observed in extreme emotional state immediately before death. ME finds no organic cause in any of the three.

VICTIMS

SUBJECT 1 — DANIEL J. SCHROEDER

M / 41 / Hampden / construction estimator / no priors

Location: Rusty's Tavern, 1117 E. Pratt St. Time: Tuesday 18 Oct, ~20:55. Cause: cardiac arrest during sustained laughter. Patron of approx. seven years.

Circumstances: Subject received phone call at 20:47 from sister M. Schroeder confirming mother had survived surgery. Began laughing. Did not stop. Patrons in bar reported being unable to refrain from laughing in his presence. Subject collapsed at 20:53. Pronounced at scene 21:02.

"He was laughing the way you laugh at the best joke you've ever heard. The whole bar was laughing with him. I dialled nine-one-one three times. The dispatcher couldn't make me out because I was laughing too. I have been working this bar nineteen years. I have never seen anything like it. I dream about it. I dream that I was happy."
— T. Halloway, bartender, statement of 19 Oct
ME (Dr. M. Erikson): Heart simply stopped. No occlusion, no infarct, no toxicology hit. The man was not unhealthy. I have nothing. Please go away. — M.E.

SUBJECT 2 — ELEANOR MAY WHITMORE

F / 67 / Federal Hill / retired schoolteacher / widow

Location: Public bench, terminus of Pier 5. Time: Wednesday 19 Oct, between 14:00 and 16:00. Cause: none identified. Heart stopped.

Circumstances: Subject discovered at 16:08 by passing tourist couple, slumped sideways, no signs of struggle. Bench is the same location subject visited regularly since husband D. Whitmore's death (2004). Bronze plaque on bench reads "for those who watch the water." No witnesses to event.

Subject's daughter (J. Whitmore-Adler, NY): "My mother went there every Friday to talk to my father. She was there on a Wednesday because of a doctor's appointment that moved her week. She was the most peaceful person I knew. If she was going to go anywhere, she was going to go on that bench. I don't know whether to be grateful or angry."

ME: No infarct. No embolism. No haemorrhage. The vasculature is fine. I cannot tell you what stopped this woman's heart. — M.E.

SUBJECT 3 — PETER J. HALLORAN

M / 38 / Dundalk / dockworker, ILA Local 333 / Hosokawa quay night shift

Location: Hosokawa quay, between container stacks D-7 and D-8. Time: Thursday 20 Oct, ~23:20. Cause: none identified. Heart stopped during emotional incident with shift supervisor F. Avila.

Circumstances: Subject and supervisor were arguing over manifest discrepancy. Argument was overheard on dispatch radio by witness M. Costa (~30m). Argument transitioned to mutual weeping and embrace. Subject dropped first. Supervisor remained holding subject for approximately four minutes before dispatch arrived.

"Pete and Frank had not been on speaking terms for six months. They were screaming at each other and then they were not. I saw them through the alley between D-7 and D-8 and they were holding each other like they were brothers. Then I saw something else, behind them, in the fog. I have not been back to D-7 since."
— M. Costa, dockworker, statement of 21 Oct

Supervisor F. Avila (currently on stress leave, declined formal interview): "I loved that asshole. I loved him. I don't know how. I have been saying it for a day and a night. My wife wants me to stop."

ME: Same as the other two. Healthy man. Heart stopped. I have run out of ways to write this. — M.E.

INVESTIGATING OFFICER'S NOTES

The medical examiner has nothing. The witnesses have nothing they can articulate. Three subjects, three locations, three demographics, no overlap except waterfront proximity and emotional intensity at time of death.

I am attaching M. Costa's statement in full despite my captain's instructions. She saw something at the third scene. I believe her. I do not have a frame for what I believe.

I have visited Pier 5 and Rusty's myself off-duty. I do not recommend others do the same.

— Det. R. Pulaski, 21 Oct 2005, 18:30
