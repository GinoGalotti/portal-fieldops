原文 — JAPANESE ORIGINAL (excerpt, paraphrased):
松田様

この樽をお送りするにあたり、ご厚情への感謝と、お詫びを申し上げねばなりません。仕込みは清くありませんでした。娘の久子が病に伏せていた間に造られたもので、米を絞る前に逝ってしまいました。私は止められませんでした。止めるべきでした。この樽は娘を運んでいると、私は思います。
— English translation by Y. Tanaka, Hosokawa Cultural Coordinator —

To Matsuda-san,

I send this cask with thanks for your hospitality and with apologies. The brewing was not clean.

My daughter Hisako was sick during the months I made it, and she died before the rice was pressed. I could not stop. I should have stopped.

The cask carries her, I think. I do not know how to say this in language fit for a business letter. Please use it gently. Or do not use it. Whichever feels right.

If you find it strange, give it to the sea. The sea took my father and his father. It will know what to do.

I am sorry. Thank you. I am sorry.

— Akira Tachibana
Tachibana Brewery, Hyōgo Prefecture
八月二十二日 / 22 August 2005
