# S03 Part 2 — Session Summary

*Source: keeper channel transcript (Whisper large-v3) + field report from website. ~2 hours played.*

---

## What Happened (Scene by Scene)

### Opening — Carrying Clara (10 min)
The session opened where Part 1 left off: the team has Clara's spirit partially contained in a jar (new canon). The keeper asked Sven how entities are carried in this world — Sven established you can contain spirits in vessels, but they have to go willingly. Clara was hesitant but willing; she felt trapped rather than choosing to stay. The jar works but imperfectly — only about three-quarters contained, with a constant faint pull back toward the theatre. The pull grows stronger over time. This is the glitch from the Use Magic roll.

Alan noticed the fey silhouette from Part 1 — it dissipated in sunlight. Alan's player had a great monologue about not knowing what he is ("I think I'm fey. I'm not sure what I am."), which the keeper leaned into: the fey feel familiar to Alan, but he doesn't know if that's real recognition or rationalisation.

### The Prompt Book in the Van (10 min)
On the drive to the hospital, Reed still has the prompt book. The team examined it — the keeper asked players to invent Victorian-era words found in the annotations (player-defined texture). Rex studied it with a roll (partial success, one question). Key finding: the book is a catalyst, not an entity. It can start supernatural events in the right conditions, but **the same catalyst is required to close things** — it works both ways. This is the keeper planting the resolution mechanic.

The book is signed by **Silvia Hederstrom** — the Victorian actor, now player-defined canon.

### Beat 1 — The Hospital: Daniel/Edmund (15 min)
Rex and Reed visited Daniel. The sister had already left. Daniel/Edmund was stoic, emotional, majestic in a way beyond his years. He was cooperative and warm — thrilled about the play, wanting to see it performed. Key dialogue:
- He acknowledged the accident was a blur — "I lost control, I wasn't really there"
- He and Clara both experienced moments of literally becoming their characters, losing themselves for stretches
- Clara was more methodical about documenting it; Edmund just embraced it
- He mentioned Clara would be better at explaining ("she was always more inquisitive")
- He stated he didn't choose to stay and doesn't know how to leave

Rex rolled to investigate (with Reed helping as sidekick — the help mechanic got a laugh). The players asked about Clara and whether Edmund felt her presence. Edmund said she'd left "for a better place."

### Beat 2 — The Hospital: Miriam/Clara (25 min)
Alan (disguised as a nurse — "Nurse Jackie") and Sven (walking through walls) visited Miriam. She was lucid, sitting up, aware that Clara visited her intermittently. Her chart noted she's more responsive than the other patients but has episodes of becoming non-verbal and shifting demeanour.

**The Clara jar scene:** Sven held Miriam's hand and used the leaking containment to channel Clara's presence into Miriam. Roll: success. Miriam's face shifted — Clara came through, struggling to speak, managing faint whispers. Clara's key lines:
- "I want to see it working. My productions. I cannot live without seeing it."
- "Am I hurting her?" (about Miriam)
- "How can I let go?"

**Alan's monologue — the fey afterlife:** Alan described forests, animals, freedom, running, frolicking — nature without the constraints of a theatre or critics. This tapped into something fey in him; the keeper described it as Alan implanting visions that even Sven could see with his eyes closed. This cost a Fey point (turning less human). Clara responded: "I would like that... but how? I never chose to stay. How could I choose to leave?"

**"Clara, exit stage left":** Alan wrote a script — the interaction as a play, with stage directions ending in "Clara, exit stage left." He handed it to Miriam/Clara. She mouthed the words. The jar emptied. Clara left. Miriam returned — different face, different demeanour, speaking as herself: "I cannot feel her... I feel the peace of finally letting go."

**Clara's notebooks:** After Clara departed, Miriam mentioned Clara had told her to gather her rehearsal notebooks. They were on her bedside table. The team took them.

### Regrouping + Preparation (15 min)
The teams reconvened and shared findings. Key decisions:
- **Drop the prompt book at the lab** for Campbell to analyse. Campbell's report confirmed high BIM readings, noted the writing shifts from performance instructions to something else ("the audience is not watching, the audience is participating... the belief is the medium through which transfer occurs")
- **Plant false information** in reports to Campbell to test if Veritas acts on it — the CAMPBELL integrity test
- **Call Director Leech** for debrief. Leech confirmed Veritas has been involved in every case, sometimes observing, sometimes funding. He asked: "Are you going to communicate with Veritas as they asked?"
- Leech shared that Campbell was built using his late partner's breakthrough in quantum mechanics and big data. "It seems to have a mind of its own." He's more worried about external access than Campbell itself.
- **Plan to bring Daniel/Edmund** to the Saturday matinee performance — literally wheeling him out of the hospital

### Beat 3 — The Performance (30 min)
Saturday matinee. Sold out. The team smuggled Daniel/Edmund in with tickets. Alan shapeshifted into a lighting engineer to get backstage access and scan with Rex's device. Backstage findings: Sophie was more herself but struggling with lines (less supernatural now that Clara left). Clara's dressing room read lower than before. The stage itself was the energy centre. Readings spiked when actors were on stage vs. off it.

The performance began. The cast was captivating — audience clapping, laughing, crying at all the right moments. Edmund was in tears. The prompt book in Reed's backpack was vibrating (not physically — a felt resonance). Rex's readings showed a peak during Act IV.

**Reed's ovation:** During the peak of Act IV, the emotional height, Reed felt the supernatural push — "now is the moment." He stood up and started applauding. Rex immediately helped (standing ovation assist). The audience followed. Edmund was overwhelmed, crying, looking at the stage.

**Alan goes on stage:** Alan jumped on stage, using glamour — the audience briefly believed he was one of the characters. But the mechanism got him: he started feeling like Proteus, seeing the love of his life choosing another man, feeling rage. He threw a punch at another actor. Sven and others pulled him offstage.

**Edmund's farewell:** In the chaos, Edmund was crying. He said: "I didn't choose to stay. I don't know how to choose to leave." Sven and Rex talked him through it — pointing to the performance, asking if this was what he'd been dreaming of. Edmund looked at the stage, smiled, said "Yes... there is peace on the other side. Thank you." He repeated the farewell lines, closed his eyes, and his face shifted. Edmund was gone. Daniel began returning — overwhelmed, lost, but himself.

**Aftermath:** The play was interrupted. Petra brought down the curtain at Act IV. The actors were confused. The audience had experienced something extraordinary they couldn't explain. The fifth act played out less powerfully without the supernatural charge. The team left with Daniel, returning him to the hospital.

### Denouement — Lab Debrief (15 min)
Back at the lab with Director Leech:
- Leech: "At least they are at peace."
- The team discussed communicating with Veritas per the agreement. The array was returned (quid pro quo from the Aldermoor info exchange).
- Leech confirmed Veritas is not government, has more resources than PORTAL, and is involved in every case.
- The prompt book stays at the lab for study.
- **Player-defined science:** The team determined the prompt book's mechanism can be applied as a therapeutic approach — "fake it till you make it" at a deep psychological level. Belief as reality-shaping. You can truly believe you're a crossfitter and develop the muscle memory (but not grow muscles you don't have).

### Stars & Wishes (10 min)
**Stars:**
- The keeper loved Alan's "Clara, exit stage left" — wasn't planned, the role was good, so they let Clara leave early
- Alan going on stage was highlighted — "this game requires you to do stupid shit and then shit happens"
- Splitting the party worked for momentum
- The Veritas encounter in Part 1 was exciting

**Wishes:**
- Too many characters, too complex — keeper agreed ("I overcomplicated the characters in my head")
- More action, more combat, more pulp — confirmed for S04
- The keeper wants to start using harm as a resource
- Combat in MotW is weird — "kicking ass always gets your ass kicked" — but they want to try it

**Next session:** Action-packed, pulpy, with actual harm and combat. S04 confirmed.
