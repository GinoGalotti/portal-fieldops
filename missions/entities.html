// CONFIRMED · RESOLVED · SESSION 01
Eszter
KELLER UNIVERSITY · CAMPBELL REPORT #0047-C · EXECUTIONER
RESOLVED — PEACEFUL DISPERSAL EXECUTIONER ASH ORIGIN — HUNGARIAN PROJECT VEIL FLAG
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// TYPE
Executioner — spirit anchored to a promise made at the moment of death. Not malicious. Protecting someone she loved.
// HARM CAPACITY
10// WILL NOT KILL BY CHOICE
CHOSE TO LEAVE MARCUS ALIVE
// ARMOUR
2-armour (incorporeal) — mundane weapons pass through her entirely. Physical harm is essentially impossible unless she is partially manifest.
// ORIGIN
Died in a road accident. Ash locket — her remains, held by Bálint Kovács. The locket was her anchor to the physical world.
// ATTACK — SPECTRAL EMBRACE
2-harm close cold intimate — wraps around a target like a freezing wind. Leaves frost on skin and bruising in the shape of handprints. Targets individuals who have harmed Bálint.
// ATTACK — HAUNTING SURGE
3-harm far ignore-armour magic — screams through electronics. Phones explode with static, screens crack, lights shatter. Target feels her grief as a physical blow. Gains area tag if she witnesses someone physically attack Bálint.
// WEAKNESS
The locket, destroyed or its ash scattered with a proper farewell. Destruction without consent disperses her violently — not restfully. Correct resolution: Bálint says goodbye of his own will. Cannot harm anyone who has never hurt Bálint unless directly attacked first.
// MINIONS — STUDENT ROBOTS (4)
5 harm each · 1-armour · Chassis Slam: 2-harm close forceful. Drill Attachment: 3-harm intimate (restrained only). Weakness: shared wireless signal with Eszter — cut power or destroy the screen and they go offline immediately.
// CUSTOM MOVES
A Promise is a Promise
Whenever Bálint is hurt, humiliated, or in genuine danger, Eszter immediately knows — regardless of distance. She will come. The Keeper may trigger her arrival at any dramatically appropriate moment after Bálint suffers harm or a serious social wound.
She Remembers Their Faces
Once Eszter has witnessed someone harm Bálint, she marks them. Marked individuals begin experiencing her grief second-hand: cold drafts, the smell of ash, waking dreams of the crash. They take –1 ongoing on all rolls while alone. Cannot be removed until Eszter is put to rest.
Let Me Go, Bálint
If Bálint sincerely asks Eszter to stop and says goodbye, she pauses. All hunters gain +1 forward on any move to put her to rest before she can resume. Window lasts one scene only.
// RESOLUTION — SESSION 01
Outcome: Humane — peaceful dispersal. Bálint said goodbye of his own will. Eszter departed restfully. The locket opened; the ash dispersed in the shape of a figure. Temperature normalised. Robots inert.

Lab directive: partial. Locket not recovered. Trace particulate collected incidentally from the scene. No conscious tissue sample obtained.
// PROJECT VEIL FLAG
Ash analysis reveals anomalous interaction with electronic systems — consistent with Haunting Surge mechanism. CAMPBELL's private note to Dr. Leech: "The ash is not inert. It retains a signal interaction property even post-dispersal. If a sample of this purity could be stabilised, the implications for Project Veil are significant."

Field team does not know this.

The Cartographer
ALDERMOOR DISTRICT · CAMPBELL REPORT #0094-B · COLLECTOR (URBAN SPRAWLER RESKIN)
ACTIVE — SESSION 02 UNRESOLVED COLLECTOR · SPATIAL PRE-HUMAN CONSCIOUSNESS PROJECT VEIL — LEECH FLAGGED MESA OBSERVED — BUYING LAND
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// TYPE
Collector (Urban Sprawler reskin, Deck of Monsters #49) — pre-human consciousness that predates the neighbourhood. Feeds on cognitive familiarity. Not malicious. Lonely.
// HARM CAPACITY
10// WILL NOT INITIATE HARM
DOES NOT UNDERSTAND WHAT IT COSTS
// ARMOUR
None — but physically invulnerable. Cannot be harmed by physical means. Immune to conventional weapons entirely. Harm must be done through communication or severing its anchor.
// MOTIVATION
To be known as completely as it once was. It was symbiotic for decades — the neighbourhood knowing itself fed the entity. The excavation broke its containment and it expanded back without understanding what changed.
// ATTACK — DISPLACEMENT
2-harm (disorientation) — targets the hunter's sense of place, not their body. On 10+: hunter temporarily lost within the zone. On 7–9: hunter loses their anchor for one scene. On a miss: permanent mild disorientation (–1 on navigation-based moves for remainder of session).
// WEAKNESS — COMMUNICATION
Any hunter who makes genuine contact — by voluntarily removing their anchor — can affect the entity with a Use Magic or Act Under Pressure roll. It does not fight someone genuinely trying to understand it. Contact is an act of vulnerability, not combat. Recovery is not guaranteed if the anchor is lost.
// SPECIALS
Animals immune — navigate by scent, not cognitive map. Unaffected. · Sven immune to displacement (no cognitive map to disrupt), not to contact — entity will attempt to communicate if he remains still. · Alan may receive fragmentary impressions without initiating contact. · The entity is everywhere in the zone at once — cannot be found in a single location.
// CUSTOM MOVES
The Palimpsest
Near the epicentre, the entity's memory of the street is faintly visible overlaid on the physical one: earlier versions of the neighbourhood — a terrace where a garden used to be, a corner where a phone box stood decades ago. Not dangerous on its own. Profoundly disorienting if a hunter's anchor slips at the same moment.
The Longer You've Lived Here
The effect scales with familiarity. New arrivals (moved in within 18 months) feel nothing unusual. A resident of 6 years disorients within 2 hours. Arthur Okafor — 41 years on Fenwick Road — disorients within minutes. When the Keeper needs to calibrate a resident's vulnerability, use depth of familiarity, not time inside the zone.
It Knows the Hunters Now
After genuine contact is made — especially if Sven shares what it's like to exist without a body — the entity will recognise the hunters in future encounters. It will not harm them. It may, in a future session, attempt to communicate from a distance: a felt wrongness that isn't disorientation, more like a nudge.
// RESOLUTION PATHS — SESSION 02
Best: Contact made, entity understands what it's doing. Retracts. Residents' familiarity returns gradually. Diane escapes. Margaret stays — wants to. Entity quieter but present.

Partial: Entity contained but not understood. Familiarity loss permanent for some long-term residents. Diane rescued. CAMPBELL can study it.

Bad: Violent attempt to destroy entity. It contracts rapidly — familiarity pulled back at once. Diane cannot be extracted. Margaret's sense of place collapses. Neighbourhood uninhabitable.
// PROJECT VEIL FLAG & MESA THREAD
CAMPBELL private note to Dr. Leech: "Topographical anchoring. If controllable, this is a delivery mechanism." Leech has not shared this with the field team.

MESA had 34% of district addresses purchased via shell companies before the first incident was reported. They knew this was coming. They were waiting for the district to empty. Reference: Greyfield Assets, Veritas Realty, Solstice Property Group — all Jersey-registered, same beneficial owner chain.

CLASS 2SPIRIT · DUTY-BOUND · CASE D ACTIVE — THE INHERITANCE
CASE D — THE INHERITANCE SPIRIT / ANCHOR TYPE COMMUNICATES BY LETTER
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// CLASSIFICATION
A consciousness anchored not by love or grief but by unfulfilled professional obligation. The anchor is the incomplete task — not a person, not an object, but a duty with no one left to discharge it. Distinct from a standard spirit in that the entity has no interest in the hunters; it is trying to finish what it started, and the hunters are either useful or irrelevant.

Martin Wentworth (Case D — The Inheritance) is the active instance. Probate lawyer, deceased 22 days. Continues writing letters to clients whose affairs he has not yet closed. Most recent letter describes an imminent killing. He believes his cardiac event was arranged. Communicates only through formal correspondence.
// ENGAGEMENT NOTES
How to reach him: Write a formal letter to his office address. He responds within 24 hours. He is precise, polite, and very difficult to emotionally move — he is not here because he feels things, he is here because a job is unfinished.

What he knows: The Meridian researcher's methodology archive — the estate he was closing when he died — and who closed it two days after his death. He does not know they arranged his death. He suspects it. He will share what he knows in exchange for assurance that his remaining clients are protected.

Performance Anchor
CLASS 2SPIRIT · ROLE-BOUND · CASE E ACTIVE — THE UNDERSTUDIES
CASE E — THE UNDERSTUDIES MESA FUNDED — THIN BOUNDARY DISPLACING LIVING PERFORMERS
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// CLASSIFICATION
Actors Clara Voss and Edmund Farrow died 6 months apart. The theatre, funded by Solstice Property Group (MESA shell company), was a thin-boundary environment — they could not fully leave when they died. They are still performing their roles. They do not know they are displacing the living understudies who stepped into those same parts.

Mechanism: the role itself is the anchor. Not the person, not the place, but the specific character performed in a specific space over a sustained period. The role has a shape in the thin-boundary environment, and both the dead and living performers are trying to inhabit the same shape.
// RESOLUTION NOTE
A theatrical farewell within the logic of performance. Clara and Edmund need to be convinced to let go — not through argument or force, but through something that makes sense within the world of the play. The scene is its own climax. Saturday performance is the window.

Sven is the obvious vector — peer conversation, something they'd actually believe. He knows what it's like to be present without being quite there. He can tell them what "letting go" means from experience without it being a speech about death.

Rex will want to study the mechanism. Sven will recognise something in Gordon — the inverse of his own condition. The hunters will have to decide whether the right move is intervention, documentation, or both.
The Replacer
CLASS 3DOPPELGANGER · IDENTITY PREDATOR · THEORETICAL — NO ACTIVE CASE
DECK OF MONSTERS — DOPPELGANGER ALAN HOOK — FEY ORIGIN NO ACTIVE CASE
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// CLASSIFICATION
An entity that replaces a human being — taking their place in their social environment, family, and identity. Not a shapeshifter in the crude sense; the replacement is a gradual assumption, identity layered over identity over time. The original is displaced rather than destroyed.

Fey connection: In some folkloric traditions, the replacer and the changeling are the same phenomenon seen from opposite ends — one is the thing left behind when a child is taken; one is the thing the child became. Alan Frazier was swapped at birth. The entity that took his place in the human world is still out there. Whether it knows Alan is now aware is unclear.
// KEEPER NOTE
This entry connects directly to Alan's fey origin thread. The entity that replaced him has been living a human life for approximately the same number of years Alan has. It may have built a family. It may not know what it is. The confrontation — when it comes — is not a fight. It is two people who are each other's shadow trying to decide what to do with that.

CLASS 3HUNGER ENTITY · CONSUMPTION · THEORETICAL — MESA THREAD
POSSIBLE MESA CREATION DECK OF MONSTERS — HUNGRY GHOST NO ACTIVE CASE
[ EXPAND ▾ ]

A consciousness that was anchored through a thin-boundary experiment but failed to maintain coherence — the result is not a stable entity but a hunger. No memory, no motivation, no communication. FEY ORIGIN — ALAN THREAD THIN BOUNDARY LOCATIONS NO ACTIVE CASE — LONG THREAD

An entity that exists specifically at the boundary between planes — not a native of either, but the space between. The Mirror
CLASS 2REFLECTION ENTITY · SELF-ORIGIN · THEORETICAL — NO ACTIVE CASE
DECK OF MONSTERS — DARK REFLECTION SELF-ORIGIN ANCHOR POTENTIAL: REX OR SVEN HOOK
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// CLASSIFICATION
An entity that originates from a person's suppressed or unacknowledged self — not a ghost or a spirit in the conventional sense, but a fragment of identity that separated and gained independent existence. Anchored to the original person by a psychological blind spot: the entity can only persist while the original refuses to acknowledge what it represents.

Typically manifests in environments of extreme self-suppression or radical identity transformation. Possible relevance: someone who reinvented themselves after a near-death experience. Someone who built an entirely new identity around their work. Someone who died and came back as something else and refuses to examine what they lost.
// KEEPER NOTE
Sven is the obvious candidate — the person he was before the ayahuasca trip and the person he is now are not the same. He has not examined that gap. A Mirror originating from that gap would be drawn to him specifically, and would manifest as whoever he was trying to become before he died.

Rex is a secondary candidate — his serial killer expertise as a "background" for a person who studies weaponised violence is its own kind of suppressed material. The thing Rex looks at professionally is a thing he chose to understand rather than feel. That's a shape a Mirror could take.
