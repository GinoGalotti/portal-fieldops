A Promise is a Promise
// KELLER UNIVERSITY · CAMPBELL REPORT #0047-C · CLASS 2 ECTOPLASMIC RESIDUE
COMPLETED ENTITY: ESZTER — EXECUTIONER DIRECTIVE: PARTIAL — ASH RECOVERED MORAL TENSION: HIGH ONE-SHOT
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// HOOK & DIRECTIVE
CAMPBELL flagged an unknown particulate at three Keller University incident scenes 23 days ago — rated low priority, sat unread. Marcus Webb is now in hospital (alive — Eszter chose to leave him that way). CAMPBELL classification: Class 2 Ectoplasmic Residue, spirit-origin, 99th percentile purity. No database match.

Lab directive: Acquire a sample. The Director believes it could advance Project Veil by 18–24 months. Escalating pressure across three checkpoints: mid Scene 1 (Director calls), after Scene 2 (requests courier), before Scene 3 (orders the locket returned).

The moral weight: The sample is the locket. The locket belongs to a grieving boy. Two good things in genuine conflict.
// SCENE SEQUENCE
Cold Open: Marcus Webb in the car park. Atmospheric dread → violence → silence. Sets the stakes. No player action.

Scene 1 — Campus: Procedural. Victim pattern links to Bálint. Ash signature, cold spots, disciplinary file. Key NPCs: Vasquez (cop, wants rational answers), Morales (professor, fond of Bálint), Priya (witness, needs empathy to open up).

Scene 2 — Dorm Room: Grief inventory. Shrine, warm locket, unsent message to his mother, wall map annotated "miért" (why), physics notes with "Eszter please go away" in the margins. Hunters stop seeing a suspect.

Interlude — Footbridge: Bálint. He knows. He's been waiting. His ask: let him say goodbye.

Scene 3 — Robotics Lab: Four robots in a circle. Screen types before anything attacks. De-escalation window first. If Bálint present: robots freeze, goodbye scene, megyek.

Something That Wants to Be Known
// ALDERMOOR DISTRICT · CAMPBELL REPORT #0094-B · TOPOGRAPHICAL DISTORTION CLASS 2
ACTIVE ENTITY: UNCLASSIFIED — SPATIAL DIRECTIVE: ESTABLISH PERIMETER · IDENTIFY ENTITY MORAL TENSION: MEDIUM RIVAL FACTION THREAD
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// HOOK & DIRECTIVE
Seven navigation failure reports in Aldermoor District over 14 days. One missing person (day 9). One hospitalisation (dissociative episode). CAMPBELL flags topographical distortion expanding at ~0.02mi/week. Epicentre: 14 Aldermoor Crescent — resident uncontactable for 9 days.

Lab directive: Do not enter affected zone without anchor protocol. Identify entity. Assess containment. The Director has a secondary ask: find out who has been buying up the district — 34% of addresses changed hands in 18 months, via shell companies, before the first incident was reported.

The rival thread: The property acquisitions trace back to the same group behind Meridian BioSciences. They knew this was coming. They were waiting for the district to empty.
// THE ENTITY
Urban Sprawler reskin (Deck of Monsters #49). Not an entity that arrived — one that grew from a disturbance during construction work 18 months ago. Feeds on familiarity. Rewrites the felt geography — the instinctive mental map residents carry — without altering physical streets.

Key tone note: It does not intend harm. It is lonely. It wants to be known. It is mapping itself onto the neighbourhood the way the neighbourhood was once mapped onto the people who lived there. Whether the hunters understand this before they try to destroy it is the moral weight of the session.

Project Veil flag: CAMPBELL's private note to Dr. Leech: "Topographical anchoring. If controllable, this is a delivery mechanism." Leech has not shared this with the field team.
// SCENE IDEAS — TO BE FLESHED OUT
Entry point: Hunters must establish an anchor — a person, object, or memory strong enough to orient them inside the zone. If they lose it, they're lost too.

Residents: Long-term locals who can't leave (get lost trying), new arrivals unaffected (no familiarity to steal yet), and the person at 14 Aldermoor Crescent who has been at the epicentre longest.

Shell company thread: A recently vacated estate agent's office. Files left behind. Someone was tracking the distortion's expansion and buying ahead of it.

The missing person: Are they lost in the zone, or did the entity absorb them into its map?
// RESOLUTION PATHS — DRAFT
Destroy it: Possible — but the entity may be the only thing that knows where the missing person is.

Communicate with it: High difficulty. Requires someone willing to go deep into the zone without an anchor.

Contain it: Lab directive preference. CAMPBELL believes it could be studied. The Director believes it could be useful.

Lab wins: Entity somehow anchored or sampled. Project Veil advances. The rival group notices PORTAL has the same asset they've been waiting to acquire.

The Understudies
// MERIDIAN THEATRE · CAMPBELL REPORT #0099-D · IDENTITY DISPLACEMENT — ROLE-ANCHORED
ACTIVE ENTITY: ROLE-ANCHORED CONSCIOUSNESS DIRECTIVE: RESOLVE BEFORE SATURDAY CURTAIN MORAL TENSION: HIGH RIVAL FACTION THREAD
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// HOOK & DIRECTIVE
Three understudies at Meridian Theatre Company hospitalised in one week — complete identity loss, responding only to their character names. The lead actors they replaced died eight months apart. Four understudies are still performing. There is a sold-out show tomorrow night.

Lab directive: Identify the mechanism. Resolve before Saturday performance if possible — PORTAL has no authority to cancel the show. Document the prompt book and the BIM array data before any resolution attempt. The Director has a secondary ask, off-CAMPBELL: follow the Solstice Property Group grant — it's structured identically to the Aldermoor acquisitions.

The MESA thread: Rook is already in the building. Three BIM Collection Arrays have been running in the lighting rig for eight months. MESA knew about this site months before PORTAL did.
// THE ENTITY — CLARA VOSS & EDMUND FARROW
Dusk reskin (role-anchored consciousness). Clara Voss and Edmund Farrow — the deceased lead actors — are present in the building as role-anchored consciousnesses. They transferred during performance into the understudies playing their roles. The mechanism: the 19th-century prompt book in the foyer is BIM-saturated to an extreme degree; audience belief amplifies it; performance context lowers the threshold for consciousness transfer.

Clara: Has been in the theatre longest. Knows more about her situation than Edmund does. Has a question she hasn't been able to ask anyone — Sven is the first person she has met who might understand the answer.

Edmund: Warm, cooperative, fully inhabited. Does not experience his situation as a problem. His contentment is the most unsettling thing about him. Clara finds it maddening.

Key tone: Resolution requires consent, not force. They chose to be here. Whether they understood what that would cost is the moral weight of the session.
// THREE INVESTIGATION ROUTES
Route A — The Hospital: Edmund (in Daniel Ashworth, Ward C) explains the mechanism from inside — cooperatively, warmly, without distress. Clara's rehearsal notebooks are accessible via the hospitalised understudies; they document the mechanism from the inside and reveal that the fictional character entered Clara before Clara entered the understudies.

Route B — The Rehearsal: The remaining understudies are still themselves but frightened. Watching rehearsal reveals the BIM arrays in the lighting rig. Rex's scanner shows partial displacement already beginning in Sophie Marsh.

Route C — The Artistic Director's Office: Grant documentation from Solstice Property Group — the acquisition appendix specifically lists the prompt book. MESA internal document misfiled. Marcus Yuen has a photograph from the donor reception — Agatha Dell, looking directly at the camera, standing near the prompt book case. First image of MESA leadership the hunters will have.
// RESOLUTION PATHS
Theatrical farewell (best): Sven goes onstage during the performance. The prompt book is on stage. Clara chooses to say goodbye within the performance logic. Edmund follows her. Understudies recover. CAMPBELL's private note: the mechanism is replicable. He is not including it in the operational report.

Force the exit: Anchor Spike breaks the contact — 3-harm to entity, forces Clara out. Understudies survive. Clara and Edmund are gone without choosing it. The toll is the question of whether they had the right to stay.

MESA takes the data and leaves: Rook's offer — full array data for PORTAL cooperation. MESA documents the mechanism and withdraws. Entity still active. Saturday's performance still happens. What the hunters do with that is their call.

The Happiest We Have Ever Been
// INNER HARBOR · CAMPBELL REPORT #0104-A · RESIDUAL CONSCIOUSNESS — NATURAL BIM SOURCE
ACTIVE ENTITY: VESSEL-BOUND SEA SPIRIT DIRECTIVE: RESOLVE BEFORE FOURTH DEATH RIVAL FACTION: VERITAS (OP. WHITECEDAR)
[ EXPAND ▾ ]
// HOOK & DIRECTIVE
Three people dead in five days at Baltimore's Inner Harbor — bartender, retiree, dockworker. ME finding: no cause. A Shōjō — Japanese sea spirit, vessel-bound — arrived eight weeks ago in a grief-brewed sake cask shipped by Hosokawa Shipping. It feeds on human emotion when the sake runs low. A fourth victim will die tonight. Six ceremonial casks on a floating barge; one is cursed. Veritas (Op. WHITECEDAR) pre-shaped the situation and wants the cask as a natural BIM source.

Lab directive: Identify the entity. Locate and secure the Tachibana cask before Veritas does. Resolve without further casualties. Document the natural-source BIM mechanism.
// THE ENTITY — THE SHŌJŌ
Cannot leave the cask. Cannot stop drinking. When sake runs low, it feeds on human emotion — the bystander cascade produces extreme euphoria, then cardiac arrest. BIM signature: muted by day, readable at close range only; spikes at dusk when it surfaces from the harbour.

Resolution key: The brewer's letter (Akira Tachibana, sealed, in Japanese). Yuki Tanaka — cultural coordinator — has it. Reading it aloud with genuine understanding of what Tachibana lost reduces the creature's armour and opens the Drink With the Spirit move. Best ending: offer sake in a cup with real emotion. The Shōjō departs. Hisako goes with it.
// THREE INVESTIGATION ROUTES
Det. Pulaski's case file: three deaths, three ME no-cause findings, one witness (Mariana Costa). Pulaski visited the death sites himself. He believes Mariana.

The brewer's letter: Sealed, in Japanese, in Yuki's possession. Names the Tachibana cask and the resolution method ("give it to the sea").

The Veritas tablet: Recovered Veritas field brief — confirms Op. WHITECEDAR, Officer M.K.'s mob pre-shaping, and that Veritas wants the cask alive as a natural BIM source.
// RESOLUTION PATHS
Drink With the Spirit (best): Sake cup + brewer's letter read aloud + genuine emotion. Shōjō departs. Hisako at peace. Cask safe to handle.

Contain and return to lab (strong): Cask secured, Shōjō inside. BIM study opportunity — and a liability. Veritas will pursue.

Veritas takes the cask (partial): Civilians safe for now. Veritas acquires a live natural-source BIM vessel. Long-term consequence: unresolved.
