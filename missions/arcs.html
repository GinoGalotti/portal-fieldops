  • REED Arc III — The Private Directive
    Revelation lands hardest on Rex — the person Reed has been simultaneously protecting and betraying. Alan's moral judgment is also in play: he uses Reed as his compass, and the needle will move.

    Alan depends on Reed as moral compass before he can act on Diane Marsh's notebook. Reed's choices in Arc III bend Alan's options. If Reed is compromised, Alan's anchor is gone.

    Rex is researching the same thread from a different angle. They will converge before either is ready. Who discovers what first shapes whether this arc is Sven's triumph or Rex's grief.

    Dan Nilsson is inside MESA, and MESA is connected to Sven's death. Rex's nemesis may hold the answer Sven has been quietly looking for. Neither knows this yet.

    The target of this arc — an operative pursuing the wrong lead for the right reasons — is Keeper's call. Assign it once a candidate emerges in play. Rex and Reed are both viable.

    Rex discovering Project Veil is a whole-team moment. How it lands depends on where Reed's Arc III, Alan's Arc III, and the CAMPBELL secret all stand at that point. Don't resolve this arc in isolation.
